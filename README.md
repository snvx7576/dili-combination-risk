# DILI Combination Risk Demo

Research prototype: estimates the temporal hepatotoxic load of 2 to 5 drugs taken together, using DILIrank 2.0 hazards and pharmacokinetic peak-overlap modelling (one-compartment Bateman curves weighted by hazard and summed over time).

Outputs: peak load (Lmax), toxic AUC, time above threshold and the danger window, with per-drug hazard and structural alert notes.

Part of the project: "Beyond Single-Drug Labels: A Layered Framework for Multi-Drug Combination Hepatotoxicity Risk Prediction" (DILIrank 2.0 + FAERS + structural alerts + temporal load + machine learning).

RESEARCH PROTOTYPE ONLY. Not for clinical decision-making. Predictions are associative, not causal.
